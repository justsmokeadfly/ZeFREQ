Not a genre.
A feeling.

ZeFREQ is an independent electronic project with two distinct vocal personalities. We fuse unlikely worlds: soulful vulnerability, industrial impact and Drum & Bass speed.

Technology sets the rhythm. Human voices give it meaning. Here, silence can be louder than a drop, and a melody heavier than a guitar.
